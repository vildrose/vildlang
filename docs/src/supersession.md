# Supersession

The end goal for Vildlang, is to be partially, or fully superseded. Vildlang will mostly be considered a success when other languages take up some of the lessons from Vildlang. To facilitate this, a major goal for the language will be to will be to document some of its most major decisions, accompanying research, evidence and lessons learned. In the end Vildlang should define a list of its most major and unique decisions, formatted in a way that they are clearly laid out, described and made actionable for other languages to adopt.
